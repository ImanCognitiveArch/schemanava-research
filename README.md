# SchemaNava — Research Showcase

A public, static, educational research showcase for a separate, privately maintained computational psychology project.

**Website:** https://imancognitivearch.github.io/schemanava-research/

## Scope
- Interactive threshold/margin illustration with explicitly invented scores; NOT the SchemaNava inference engine.
- Reported local offline matched comparison of 28 synthetic items: guided 6/28 candidate emissions; free 25/28. Two repeated outputs per condition matched in the reported local environment.
- These counts are descriptive and have NOT been independently audited. They do not demonstrate accuracy, superiority or clinical validity.
- Independent blinded expert annotation and formal accuracy scoring are pending.

## Method distinction
This demonstration compares raw top-one retrieval against the same ranked candidates after a cosine similarity threshold and top-two margin filter; it is a **filter ablation**, not a complete SchemaNava rule-engine evaluation or clinical decision support.

This page makes no external requests or links to unrelated tutorial projects.
