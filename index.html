Make room for uncertainty.

A research exploration of schema-related retrieval, explicit selection rules, and the decision to abstain.

SchemaNava investigates a testable question: what changes when a local semantic retriever must meet evidence thresholds before emitting a candidate?

01 / Local experiment

Evidence over spectacle.

A reported offline matched comparison on 28 synthetic English items, using the same local model, candidate rankings and context gates. Only the top-one similarity/margin selection filter changed between arms.

28 items

Frozen synthetic dataset; 18 schema-related catalogue labels.

2× per arm

Reported exact output agreement across repeated runs in one environment.

19 different decisions

Filter retained an abstention where filter-free retrieval emitted a candidate.

Observed candidate coverage

Emissions and abstentions out of 28 items.

Rule-guided6 emitted · 22 abstained
Filter-free25 emitted · 3 abstained

21.4% versus 89.3% candidate emission. Threshold ≥ 0.40; margin ≥ 0.08.

What this does—and does not—show

With identical ranked candidates, imposing a selection filter lowered the number of candidates emitted. Reported repeated outputs matched.

Not independently validated: These are descriptive figures reported by a private local Codex run, not a published or externally audited result. No independent expert reference was available. Accuracy, superiority and clinical validity are not established.

This is an ablation of one filter, not an evaluation of the full SchemaNava engine.

03 / Research protocol

Designed to be questioned.

01 / FREEZE

Keep the 28 synthetic prompts and reference hashes fixed.

02 / MATCH

Hold local encoder, rankings and upstream context rules constant.

03 / ABLATE

Change only the threshold/margin filter, preserving raw outputs.

04 / REVIEW

Obtain independent blinded judgments before scoring correctness.

Research-only educational showcase. No diagnostic claims, psychotherapy advice, clinical deployment, personal health data, model files, hidden prompts or proprietary inference implementation are exposed.
